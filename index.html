SQA Case Study

Incident Report: DHCP Pool Exhaustion & Rogue IP Collision RCA

Defect triage diagnosing unreleased leases and address collision anomalies

Incident ID: INC-LINUX-2026-022
Severity: P2 - Address Exhaustion
Root Cause: Excessive 7-Day Lease Time
Resolution: Tuned to 8 Hours + Flush

1. Symptom

New client laptops joining the office Wi-Fi received APIPA auto-configuration addresses (`169.254.x.x`) with no network access, while existing workstations retained connectivity.

2. Root Cause Analysis

Checking `/var/lib/dhcp/dhcpd.leases` showed 101 out of 101 available addresses in the pool were marked `active`. The previous configuration had `default-lease-time` set to 7 days, holding expired guest and mobile phone leases indefinitely.

3. SQA Resolution & Preventative SOP

Tuned lease time to 8 hours (`28800` seconds), purged abandoned leases, and expanded the pool to `/23`. Zero DHCP starvation defects detected over 30 days of monitoring.
